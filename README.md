# GoldenHarvest — Dry Fruits & Nuts Store

Premium e-commerce storefront for **GoldenHarvest**, featuring single-origin almonds, jumbo cashews, organic Medjool dates, nutrient-dense nut mixes, luxury gift boxes, and bulk wholesale procurement.

## 🌟 Pages & Structure

- **Home (V1)** (`index.html`) — Hero showcase, freshness metrics, signature nut collections, 4-step harvest journey, and Loose Market vs. Export Grade interactive comparison.
- **Home (V2)** (`home2.html`) — Split-screen visual hero, interactive Custom Nut Mix & Gift Box Builder, weekly harvest carousel, Privilege Club tiers, and community feed.
- **Our Story** (`about.html`) — Farm-to-table heritage, sustainable orchard partnerships, oil-free roasting craft, and quality certifications.
- **Products** (`services.html`) — Filterable catalog by category:
  - Premium Almonds (Mamra, California Nonpareil, Spanish Marcona)
  - Jumbo Cashews (Goan W180 King, Black Pepper & Salt, Smoked Paprika)
  - Dates Collection (Royal Medjool, Madinah Ajwa, Pistachio-Stuffed)
  - Superfood Mixes (Antioxidant Berry Blend, Protein Power, Saffron Mukhwas)
- **Gift Boxes** (`gallery.html`) — Interactive packaging transformation sliders and 6 luxury gift collections (Royal Velvet, Sovereign Brass, Festive Zari, Rosewood Chest, etc.).
- **Bulk Orders** (`blog.html`) — Corporate gifting programs, HORECA & culinary supply, tiered wholesale pricing, and packaging specifications.
- **Contact Us** (`contact.html`) — Order enquiry form, flagship boutique address, roasting hours, and interactive map.
- **Enquire Form** (`enquire.html`) — Dedicated standalone product and bulk enquiry page with category selector, pack size estimators, and direct enquiry channels.

## 🎨 Theme & Palette

- **Primary**: Roasted Almond Amber (`#b45309`, `#92400e`)
- **Accent**: Wildflower Honey Gold (`#d97706`)
- **Secondary**: Pistachio Olive Green (`#4d7c0f`, `#3f6212`)
- **Background**: Warm Parchment (`#fbf8f4`) & Deep Walnut Espresso (`#140c06`)
- **Typography**: Outfit (headings) & Inter (body)
- **Features**: Dark/Light mode toggle, RTL/LTR layout toggle, mobile slide menu, responsive glassmorphism navigation.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or serve via any static web server (e.g. `npx serve`, Live Server).
