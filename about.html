Who We Are

About GoldenHarvest

Discover our orchard-to-jar heritage, our master nut roasters, and our unwavering commitment to delivering pure, wholesome nutrition to every home.

Founded in 2011

Sourcing the World's Finest Nuts & Dry Fruits for Over a Decade

GoldenHarvest was founded with a singular mission: to bridge the gap between heritage organic orchards and health-conscious families. Our founder, Rebecca Stern, noticed that most supermarket nuts were old-crop, oil-fried, or treated with sulfites. She set out to change that by building direct trade partnerships with growers in California, Kashmir, Iran, and Arabia.

Orchard-Direct Quality

We procure 100% first-harvest crops directly from certified sustainable farms.

Oil-Free Dry Roasting

Our nuts are slow-roasted in hot air ovens without palm or vegetable oils.

Wholesome For All

Naturally vegan, gluten-free, non-GMO, and rich in heart-healthy omega fats.

Our Standards

Quality Certifications & Wholesome Benchmarks

Every jar and gift box packed at GoldenHarvest adheres to strict international food safety and grading standards.

100% Organic Orchards

Grown in mineral-rich soils by partner orchards practicing sustainable, pesticide-free cultivation.

Hand-Graded Jumbo Size

Optical laser sorting combined with manual inspection guarantees king-size W180/W240 cashews and whole kernels.

Zero Sulfites or Oils

No artificial bleaching, no sulfur dioxide, and zero refined oils—just pure sun-dried fruit and dry-roasted nuts.

Vacuum-Sealed Freshness

ISO-certified nitrogen flushing and food-grade glass jars preserve natural crunch for up to 12 months.

Our Curing Craft

Small-Batch Dry Roasting and Artisanal Infusions

We believe wholesome snacking should never compromise on flavor. Our master roasters carefully calibrate temperature curves for each nut variety so almonds develop a deep toasted aroma while cashews retain their natural buttery sweetness.

Whether you prefer raw unroasted Mamra almonds for soaking, Kashmiri saffron-infused cashews, or antioxidant-rich berry trail mixes, every batch is crafted fresh to order.
