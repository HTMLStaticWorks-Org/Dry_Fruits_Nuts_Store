Why Businesses Trust GoldenHarvest Bulk Supply

Understand the technical grading and preservation standards behind our wholesale cartons and corporate hampers.

Strict Count-Per-Ounce Grading & Lab Testing

Ordinary wholesale suppliers mix crop years and kernel sizes, leading to uneven roasting and bitter nuts. At GoldenHarvest, every lot comes with a Certificate of Analysis (COA) verifying current-season harvest, moisture below 4%, and zero aflatoxins.

Whether you specify 18/20 count Nonpareil almonds or W180 king cashews, our optical calibrators ensure 99.5% size uniformity across every crate.

Multi-Layer Nitrogen Vacuum Bricks

Natural nut oils oxidize rapidly when packed in standard polybags. We seal all wholesale orders in 5-layer food-grade vacuum bricks flushed with 99.9% food-grade nitrogen.

This oxygen-free environment prevents rancidity, protects delicate kernels from transit breakage, and locks in fresh-roasted crunch for up to 12 months without artificial preservatives.

Bulk Order FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about wholesale orders, corporate branding, and bulk shipping.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for bulk orders?

Our MOQ starts at 25 boxes for custom corporate or wedding gift hampers, and 25 kg (in 5kg or 10kg vacuum packs) for loose wholesale nuts, dates, and trail mixes.

Can you customize gift boxes with our company logo and branding?

Yes! We offer gold/silver foil logo stamping, custom outer sleeves, branded satin ribbons, and custom-printed message cards on orders of 25+ gift boxes.

Can we order a sample kit before confirming a bulk purchase?

Absolutely. Corporate and wholesale buyers can request our 8-variety Executive Tasting Sample Kit via our Enquiry page, and the sample cost is fully credited toward your bulk order.

Do you ship individual gift boxes to multiple recipient addresses?

Yes. Simply share your recipient spreadsheet with our corporate team, and we will dispatch individual protective gift parcels directly to each doorstep with tracking notifications.

How long do your vacuum-packed dry fruits stay fresh?

Unopened nitrogen-flushed jars and wholesale vacuum bricks maintain peak orchard crispness for 9 to 12 months when stored in a cool, dry place. Once opened, store in an airtight container and enjoy within 6 to 8 weeks.
