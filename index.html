Orchard to Pantry

Our 4-Step Harvest Journey

We follow a rigorous farm-to-jar curation process to guarantee that every kernel delivers maximum nutrition, crunch, and natural aroma.

01

Direct Orchard Sourcing

We partner directly with certified organic growers in California, Kashmir, Iran, and Madinah for first-harvest crops.

02

Optical & Hand Grading

Every nut and date is optically sorted and hand-inspected for uniform jumbo size, zero breakage, and optimal moisture.

03

Artisan Dry Roasting

Slowly dry-roasted in small batches without a drop of refined oil, seasoned gently with Himalayan rock salt or herbs.

04

Vacuum-Sealed Fresh

Packed immediately into nitrogen-flushed pouches, glass jars, and luxury gift boxes to lock in orchard-fresh crunch.
